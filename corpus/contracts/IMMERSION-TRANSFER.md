# Immersion transfer — optional discovery note

Use only when cross-domain exploration was explicitly chosen. This is a **candidate generation** record and must not claim verified target-domain conclusions.

## Sections

### Original blocked question
What conventional explanations kept repeating?

### External source and observation
Describe what was actually perceived, learned, or practiced. Do not replace observations with metaphorical interpretation.

### Proposed structural analogy
What relation, constraint, rhythm, feedback pattern, or distinction may transfer?

### How the analogy could fail
List target-domain mismatches and possible seductive-but-false similarities.

### Target-domain hypothesis
State one falsifiable technical, organizational, or experiential claim generated by the transfer.

### Next discriminating test
What minimal test would distinguish it from a serious rival in the target domain?

### Learning level
Did this create an idea, alter the problem framing, or plausibly change a durable rule for generating future questions? Only the third is a candidate for a higher-order method change, and that still needs independent follow-up.
