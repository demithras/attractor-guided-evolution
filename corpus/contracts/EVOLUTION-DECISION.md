# Evolution decision — record contract

The owner of the project or an appropriately authorized agent chooses among retain, revise, replace, pivot, defer, stop, and escalate.

## Required sections

### Decision and scope
Say what is being decided, within which bounds, and whether it is approved or proposed.

### Evidence basis
Reference relevant experimental verdicts, constraints, and non-experimental evidence. Note missing evidence.

### Attractor alignment
State which desired capability or invariant is advanced or endangered and whether this calls for formal charter review.

### Alternatives and costs
Include a genuine simple rival where relevant. Count implementation, integration, maintenance, operations, security, human and agent costs where they matter.

### Chosen action
State concrete engineering or practice changes, including deletion, no change, and stopping work as legitimate options.

### Authority and reversibility
State who may approve, any external effects, rollback conditions, and follow-up monitoring.

### Knowledge updates
Say which hypotheses are retired, superseded, reopened, or left active, and which new decision-relevant question follows.

## Gate

Do not infer authority to act from a convincing recommendation. Core changes to purpose or invariants need explicit human approval.
