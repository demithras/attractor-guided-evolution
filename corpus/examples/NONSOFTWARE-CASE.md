# Worked case — developing musical listening as a capability

This example shows that AGE is not restricted to code or to numerical experiments.

## Attractor

Develop a more differentiated listening ability: notice spatial production, timing, silence, repetition, and timbral transformations in unfamiliar and familiar music. Preserve enjoyment and genuine exploration; do not optimize for a quiz score at the expense of attention.

## Routine development

Listen deliberately to contrasting albums and record which features can be identified and described consistently. This is direct skill practice, not necessarily a formal hypothesis spike.

## Consequential hypothesis

Exposure to dub production techniques might make delay and silence easier to detect in a different genre. The rival is that any attentive repeated listening, rather than dub specifically, would improve recognition.

## Minimal experiment

Before listening, describe what is heard in several unfamiliar excerpts. Explore the contrasting production style. Later, describe fresh excerpts matched for basic familiarity and complexity. If possible, compare with another attention-training route. Record changes and obvious biases. Do not pretend this small personal protocol has established general causality.

## Immersion transfer

The learning experience may prompt an analogy about delayed responses in distributed systems. That analogy is **not** proof of a systems property. It becomes a valid input to engineering only after a target-domain claim and experiment are specified.

## Decision

Retain the practice if it improves the desired perceptual capability and is enjoyable within the user's own criteria; adapt or stop if not. The artifact in this case is a skill, not a repository.
