# Worked case — Paladin operational ontology research

**Illustrative basis:** `demithras/operational-ontology-poc`, particularly `round2/README.md`, `round2/AGENTS.md`, and `round2/RESULTS.md`, as reviewed in October 2026. Consult the repository's current evidence before reusing exact results. This example is not a new experiment.

## Attractor

Build an executable, secure, governable operational system that preserves authority, knowledge provenance, and continuity of operation under relevant failure conditions, with justified complexity. A specific ontology engine is a **candidate means**, not the goal.

## Decision-relevant hypotheses

- Can a language represent the required operational contracts without losing typed meaning?
- Can a generic engine enforce a hard Function/Action boundary?
- Does a distinct executable ontology architecture earn its complexity against the strongest conventional baseline?

## Observed experimental pattern

Round 2 reported scoped support for some technical capabilities (including lossless OpenPona v2 representation, a Function/Action boundary after engine fixes, and generic engine/toolchain behavior). It also reported rejection of the stronger economic/value claim for the Project Ontology and of semantic-variety break-even in tested comparisons, with limitations specified in that round's results.

## AGE reading

The 'machine can work' observation does not demonstrate 'this architecture is justified'. Supporting evidence for bounded capabilities coexists with evidence against an architectural premium. A reasonable direction is to retain useful verified capabilities, challenge or discard expensive ontological machinery, and compare continued efforts against a strong conventional implementation.

## Proper decision options

- Keep proven security and action-governance invariants regardless of architecture.
- Retire or narrow an uneconomic ontology layer rather than rescuing it by renaming the goal.
- Preserve rejected and supported claims with their scopes and dependency relationships.
- If seeking a new benefit claim, preregister a genuinely new test with fair costs and baselines.

## Anti-pattern

Bad: 'All engine tests passed, therefore the ontology has strategic value.'

Better: 'The engine passed particular tests; comparative value is a separate claim and failed where examined.'
