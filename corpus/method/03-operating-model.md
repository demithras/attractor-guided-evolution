# 03 — Operating model and work routing

AGE uses three main feedback loops and one cross-cutting discovery channel. Do not create a new process tier when an existing loop already explains the work.

## Delivery loop

Use when the change and success criteria are understood. Implement the smallest safe change; test; deploy or deliver with authorization; capture meaningful new observations. Do not inflate routine engineering into pretend research.

## Experimental loop

Use when a consequential claim is uncertain. State rival explanations and the decision at stake; freeze fair test conditions; run a small discriminating experiment; record evidence and scoped verdict; decide what changes next.

## Direction loop

Use when evidence challenges priorities, invariants, or the definition of desirable outcomes. Compare possible attractor formulations and explicitly seek human authorization for core changes. A direction loop is not triggered by every rejected implementation hypothesis.

## Immersion / convergence channel

Use cross-domain observation, embodied practice, artistic experience, design analogies, or interdisciplinary reading to broaden candidate hypotheses when discovery is stuck or homogeneous. Translate each promising analogy into an explicit, independently testable domain claim. Preserve origin and limitations; do not claim the external experience proves the target-domain claim.

## Work router

Ask in order:

1. Is the task routine, well specified, and reversible? **Direct delivery.**
2. Does a contested assumption materially affect quality, cost, risk, or architecture? **Hypothesis spike.**
3. Are the criteria for calling progress 'good' themselves in conflict? **Attractor review.**
4. Is the team unable to generate useful rival explanations, or trapped in the same framing? **Optional immersion transfer**, then route the resulting claims back to a spike.
5. Is the cost of learning greater than the cost and risk of a reversible change? **Prefer a monitored small change** rather than a full formal experiment.

## Portfolio view

Do not optimize isolated spikes. Track which project decisions were improved, which assumptions were eliminated, which uncertainties remain dangerous, and whether research overhead is justified. Retire stale experiments and redundant machinery.

## Feedback discipline

Observations flow upward; approved decisions flow downward. Implementation convenience cannot silently redefine the attractor. Attractive new framings cannot retroactively change a completed verdict.
