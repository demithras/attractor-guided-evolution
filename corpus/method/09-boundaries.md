# 09 — Conceptual boundaries and research status

## Orders of change: explanatory analogy, not a forced ontology

- **Order zero:** Existing artifact.
- **First-order change:** Development modifies the artifact.
- **Second-order change:** Hypothesis-driven engineering modifies how development decisions are made.
- **Third-order change:** Attractor guidance modifies how hypotheses and directions are prioritized and interpreted.
- **Fourth-order hypothesis:** Immersion may sometimes modify how possible attractors and hypotheses are generated; often it merely supplies cross-domain input to lower-order work.
- **Orders five to seven:** No established necessity or unique operational mechanism. Do not create skills or organizational strata merely to fill these numbers.

Do not claim these are literal derivatives of source code with respect to time. Mathematical time derivatives and recursive changes in decision policy are distinct constructs.

## Research program versus protected belief

An attractor is not an excuse to keep rescuing a favored architecture. Strong negative evidence may recommend abandoning implementation assumptions or the entire pursuit. Core purpose is durable, **not immune to explicit review**.

## Evidence cannot determine ultimate values

Experiments can reveal feasibility, cost, harms, and unintended outcomes. They do not by themselves determine what is ethically desirable or who is legitimate to choose. Keep empirical and normative claims distinct.

## Distinguish four meanings of success

1. An artifact runs.
2. A narrow claim has support.
3. The approach beats a fair alternative in relevant contexts.
4. The resulting system advances an approved purpose.

Do not infer later successes from earlier ones.

## Open questions, not dogma

- Does formal attractor guidance improve long-run adaptation under uncertainty?
- Can immersion reliably generate better discriminating hypotheses than ordinary research?
- Which cost/risk regimes justify full preregistered spikes?
- Does explicit research memory lower duplicated effort as teams and agents change?
- Are further recursive orders distinct and useful, or redundant descriptions?

Treat these as a research backlog with possible falsifiers, not facts to teach a new agent as proven.
