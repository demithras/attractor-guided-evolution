# 07 — Immersion and Convergence without epistemic inflation

**Immersion Spike** is a bounded excursion into another medium, discipline, practice, or mode of attention intended to disrupt a stale problem framing and generate candidate insights.

**Convergence Praxis** is a broader practice of integrating distinct forms of experience, knowing, and acting. In AGE it supplies discovery input and may, in some cases, change how the agent or person generates future directions.

## Recommended protocol

1. State the blocked question and what routine analysis keeps repeating.
2. Choose a sufficiently different but potentially structurally relevant source domain.
3. Observe without forcing an immediate analogy.
4. Record the source observation precisely, separating what was perceived from what was inferred.
5. Propose a **transfer hypothesis**: what structural feature might generalize to the target domain?
6. Identify where the analogy may break.
7. Turn any useful transfer into a target-domain prediction and a candidate spike.
8. Evaluate against target-domain baselines. Retain or reject the claim independently of whether the immersion felt profound.
9. Record whether the excursion produced only a new idea, a new framing, or a durable change in the method of generating questions.

## Limits

A metaphor never demonstrates its own correctness. Music, martial arts, psychology, biology, mythology, or subjective sensation may inspire a model, but do not confer scientific authority on a target-domain claim.

## Is immersion a fourth 'derivative'?

**PROVISIONAL.** It is not automatically a fourth recursive order. A single new coding idea from another domain is a cross-domain input into an existing hypothesis loop. A claim of higher-order change requires evidence that the **rules for generating or selecting future directions** changed, not merely the contents of the next idea.

## Safety and human context

When immersion involves health, mental states, the body, or personally sensitive practices, avoid framing private subjective experience as objective mechanism or providing hazardous experiments. Adapt evidence standards to the domain and preserve individual consent and autonomy.
